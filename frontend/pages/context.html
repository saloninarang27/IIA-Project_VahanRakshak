🧭 Federated Context Discovery & Document Integration

The query is split into sub-queries and sent to all five agency databases, each on its own machine. Every database follows its own FK → PK relationships to discover the context around the query and returns its most relevant facts plus the keys that lead into other databases (an owner id, an FIR number…). The Mediator sends those keys to the databases that hold them in the next round, and keeps only the facts closely related to the query. That context then drives the search of the unstructured documents (FIR narratives, surveyor reports, news, tip-offs, officer notes), and what the documents say is checked against what the databases say.
